A personal portfolio website that introduces me, highlights my skills, and showcases my work — built as my final project for the HTML & CSS module of my Full Stack training.

About the Project
This site works as a live, digital version of my resume. It's a single-page site that gives visitors a quick, clear picture of who I am, what I know, and how to reach me — all in one place, without needing to open a separate document.

What's Inside
Hero — a short introduction, with links to my CV and LinkedIn
About Me — my background and technical skills
Projects — a look at my work, starting with Tour Guide, a tourism web platform
Contact — a simple way to get in touch
Footer — quick links to my LinkedIn, GitHub, and email
Built With
HTML5 and CSS3 only — no frameworks, no JavaScript.
